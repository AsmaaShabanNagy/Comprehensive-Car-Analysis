# Comprehensive-Car-Analysis
This dashboard helps stakeholders understand the car market dynamics, brand performance, and consumer preferences across regions, assisting in strategic decision making.
Comprehensive Car Analysis Dashboard

This project presents a detailed interactive dashboard for analyzing car data across different regions, brands, conditions, and years. The dashboard provides insights into car quantities, prices, popularity, and market demand using various visualizations.

Features

- Brand Analysis: Distribution and demand of different car brands.
- Regional Analysis: Car quantity and popularity across regions (Asia, Europe, USA).
- Condition Analysis: Breakdown of car conditions (new, used, salvage, restored).
- Price Insights: Average, maximum, and minimum car prices.
- Year Filter: Filter data by manufacturing years.
- Interactive Visuals: Bar charts, pie charts, and donut charts to explore data.
- Summary Metrics: Key statistics displayed on the left panel such as average price, max price, min price, total cars, and average speed.

Technologies Used

- Power BI for data visualization and dashboard creation.
- Dataset includes car sales data by brand, country, condition, and year.

How to Use

- Use the slicers at the top to filter data by brand, condition, country, and year.
- Analyze different charts for detailed insights on demand and distribution
